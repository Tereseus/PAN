Listening modes

You choose how ΠAN listens. There are five ways. Switch any time.

Normal is where ΠAN starts. Listen only suits a meeting or a film. The box at the bottom of Home and the notification say the mode in words too.

Switch with the button

  • Tap the microphone button to mute or unmute.
  • Hold it to open the list of modes, then tap one. A tick shows the one you are in.
  • The same button is in People, and the notification has Mute and Unmute.

Switch by saying so

To go toSay
Listen onlyΠAN, just listen
Name onlyΠAN, name only
Don't rememberΠAN, don't remember this or off the record
Normalback to normal or you can talk
MuteΠAN, mute or stop listening

ΠAN says a short Okay. These words are English. In other languages, use your own words or the button.

Two modes end only with the button.

Mute: the microphone is off, so ΠAN cannot hear you. Tap the button.

Don't remember: nothing you say ends it, so a stray sentence cannot switch memory back on. Tap or hold the button and pick another mode.

Your own words

In Settings, Voice and sounds, How ΠAN listens, add words in any language, one per line, then tap Save.

  • Your name for ΠAN (Name only): another name to call it by.
  • Words that end Listen only: words that let ΠAN talk again.

Calls work in every mode.
