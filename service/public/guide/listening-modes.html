Listening modes

You choose how ΠAN listens. There are five ways, and you can switch any time.

The five modes

ModeWhat it does
Normal
picture: a microphone		ΠAN hears everything and answers when it thinks you are talking to it. It remembers the talk. This is where ΠAN starts.
Listen only
picture: an ear		ΠAN hears and remembers, but says nothing. Good for a meeting or a film.
Name only
picture: Π		ΠAN answers only lines that start with its name, or with your own word for it.
Don't remember
picture: a hat with glasses		ΠAN talks as usual, but keeps nothing. Not on your phone, not on the hub. It ends by itself after an hour.
Mute
picture: a crossed out microphone		The microphone is off. ΠAN hears nothing and keeps nothing.

The picture on the microphone button shows the mode you are in. The box at the bottom of Home says it in words, for example Name only. ΠAN answers when you start with its name. The ΠAN notification says it too.

Switch with the button

  • Tap the microphone button to mute or unmute.
  • Hold the microphone button. A list opens: Normal, Listen only, Name only, Don't remember, Mute. Tap one. A tick shows the one you are in.
  • The same button is in People and in every conversation there.
  • The ΠAN notification has a Mute and Unmute button too.

Switch with your voice

To go toSay
Listen onlyΠAN, just listen or listen only
Name onlyΠAN, name only or only answer to your name
Don't rememberΠAN, don't remember this or off the record
Normalback to normal or you can talk
MuteΠAN, mute, be quiet or stop listening

ΠAN says a short Okay so you know it heard.

Two things only the button can undo.

Mute: the microphone is off, so ΠAN cannot hear you ask it to come back. Tap the microphone button.

Don't remember: nothing you say ends it, so a stray sentence cannot switch memory back on. Tap or hold the button and pick another mode. Or wait an hour, and it ends on the next thing you say.

Your own words

Go to Settings, Voice and sounds, How ΠAN listens. You can add your own words in any language, one per line, then tap Save.

  • Your name for ΠAN (Name only): a name you would rather call it. Its own name always works too.
  • Words that end Listen only: words that let ΠAN talk again. You can talk always works.

For now, the spoken commands in the table above work in English. In other languages, use your own words or the button.

Calls

Calls with family work in every mode. See People.
