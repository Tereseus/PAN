On all your devices

ΠAN is the same on every device you use: one memory, one voice. It knows which one you are at, and speaks there.

Where ΠAN is

  • Your phone, and any tablet you pair. See Getting started.
  • Your car, through your phone. See In the car.
  • Your computers. See Your computers.
  • Headphones, earbuds, glasses and the pendant, through your phone.

Messages and reminders follow you

If you set up the hub, go to Settings, Voice and sounds, Reminders and messages.

  • Follow me: they go to the device you last talked to ΠAN on.
  • Send them here: they always go to this device.

If that device is off, another of yours takes them. Nothing is lost.

What ΠAN sees of your day

Under the connection line on Home, a short line says what ΠAN thinks you are doing. Tap it for With (who is around), Mood and Lately (recent topics).

ΠAN uses the name of a place, never your exact position. To stop it using location, go to Settings, Privacy, What each device may use.

When ΠAN speaks first

  • Someone in your group sends a message and Read messages out loud is on.
  • Someone calls you.
  • Something needs you. It shows on Home under Needs you. Tap Dismiss when done.
  • Now and then ΠAN starts a short talk on its own, on the device you are at.
