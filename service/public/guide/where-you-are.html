Where ΠAN thinks you are

ΠAN keeps a simple guess of where you are and what you are doing. It uses the guess to answer better, and to pick which device should speak.

How ΠAN guesses

  • At the computer: you typed or moved the mouse in the last two minutes on a computer that has ΠAN on it.
  • In the car: your phone is on a car's Bluetooth, or it moved at car speed in the last ten minutes.
  • Out: otherwise, the place your phone was in when you last talked to ΠAN.

What you see

Under the connection line on Home, a short line says what ΠAN thinks you are doing. Tap it to see more: With (who is around), Mood and Lately (recent topics).

What ΠAN uses it for

  • Answers about your place: where am I?, is it going to rain?, when is sunset?
  • Picking the device that should speak up.

Your exact position is never put in what ΠAN reads. It uses the name of the place. To stop ΠAN using your location, turn location off under Settings, Privacy, What each device may use.

When ΠAN speaks up by itself

Most of the time ΠAN only answers. It speaks without being asked when:

  • Someone on your ΠAN sends you a message, and Read messages out loud is on.
  • Someone calls you. Your phone rings.
  • You get stuck looking for a word. ΠAN may offer it.

The hub owner can choose which of their devices reads messages and reminders out. Go to Settings, Voice and sounds, Reminders and messages. Pick Send them here for this device, or Follow me for the device you last talked to ΠAN on.

Things the hub wants you to see show on Home under Needs you. Tap Dismiss when you have read one.

ΠAN starting a short talk with you on its own, for example to suggest a break, is Coming.
