Translating

ΠAN can be the interpreter between you and someone who speaks another language.

Start

Say one of these:

  • ΠAN, be our interpreter. She speaks Spanish.
  • ΠAN, translate everything I say into Japanese.
  • ΠAN, translate for us.

ΠAN answers, for example Okay, I'll interpret. Japanese and English.

You can name the language a moment later. If you never name it, ΠAN listens to the other person's first line and works it out.

While it interprets

  • What you say in English, ΠAN says in the other language.
  • What they say, ΠAN says to you in English.
  • ΠAN translates every line and answers none of them. Do not expect it to answer questions meanwhile.
  • Ask them how much this costs: ΠAN asks them directly, in their language.
  • Say that again: ΠAN repeats its last translation.
  • The phone gets a bit louder so the other person can hear.

Home shows a badge at the top, like Interpreting: Japanese. Lines in another script, like Japanese, show Latin letters underneath so you can say them. Tap such a line to see each word, how it sounds and what it means.

Stop

  • Tap the Interpreting badge on Home.
  • Or say stop translating.
  • It also stops by itself two hours after the last line it translated.

Just one phrase

For a single phrase, ask: How do you say thank you in Japanese? or Translate good morning into Greek. ΠAN answers once and goes back to normal.
