Translating

ΠAN interprets between you and someone who speaks another language. You each talk, and you each hear your own language.

Start

  • ΠAN, be our interpreter. She speaks Spanish.
  • ΠAN, translate for us.
  • ΠAN, translate everything I say into Japanese.

ΠAN says which two languages it will use. If you never name one, it works it out from the other person's first line.

While it interprets

  • ΠAN translates every line and answers none of them.
  • Ask them how much this costs: ΠAN asks them in their language.
  • Say that again: ΠAN repeats its last line.
  • The phone gets a bit louder, so they can hear.

Home shows a badge, like Interpreting: Japanese. Lines in another script show Latin letters underneath, so you can say them. Tap a line to see each word and what it means.

Stop

Tap the Interpreting badge, or say stop translating. It also stops two hours after the last line.

When they have ΠAN too

Each phone speaks to its own person, in that person's language. You both just talk.

Just one phrase

Ask How do you say thank you in Japanese? ΠAN answers once and goes back to normal.
