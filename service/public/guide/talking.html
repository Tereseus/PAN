Talking with ΠAN

Talk the way you talk to a person. ΠAN follows the talk and joins in when you speak to it.

Its name

ΠAN sounds like "pan". In the usual mode it answers when it thinks you are talking to it. Its name at the start makes sure. Right after an answer, go on without the name.

Want answers only when you say its name? Use Name only. See Listening modes.

When it stays quiet

When talk is not for it, Home shows a slanted note under your line, like Not for ΠAN: sounded like you were talking to someone else. If it was for ΠAN, say its name and ask again.

Lose a word mid sentence, and ΠAN may offer it.

Stopping it

  • Say stop, wait or enough.
  • Or just start talking. Yeah and mm hmm do not stop it.
  • Press the phone's power button, or tap Stop talking in the notification.

Typing

Where you cannot talk, tap the box at the bottom of Home. It says Type to ΠAN. While you talk, the same box shows your words.

The voice button

The voice button on a steering wheel or headset, or your phone's voice gesture, can open ΠAN. Android may ask which app to use the first time. Pick ΠAN. After a press, the next thing you say is for ΠAN.

How ΠAN talks

Go to Settings, Voice and sounds, Personality. In How ΠAN talks, write something like "calm and short", then tap Save. Turn Off brings back the usual voice.

In the same place, Sound Effects and Vibration turn ΠAN's small sounds and buzzes on or off.
