Talking to ΠAN

Talk the way you talk to a person. ΠAN listens, and answers when you are talking to it.

Saying its name

ΠAN sounds like "pan". In the usual mode it listens to everything and answers when it thinks you are talking to it. Starting with its name makes sure: ΠAN, how far is the moon?

Right after ΠAN answers, you can go on without its name: and Mars? or how long would it take to get there?

If you only want answers when you say its name, use Name only. See Listening modes.

When ΠAN stays quiet

ΠAN keeps out of talk that is not for it, like you talking to someone in the room or music playing. Home then shows a small slanted note under your line, for example Not for ΠAN: sounded like you were talking to someone else. If it was for ΠAN, say its name and ask again.

Stopping it mid sentence

  • Say stop, wait or enough while it talks.
  • Or just start talking. A couple of real words stop it. Yeah or mm hmm do not.
  • Press the phone's power button.
  • Or pull down the ΠAN notification and tap Stop talking.

Typing instead

Where you cannot talk, tap the box at the bottom of Home. It becomes Type to ΠAN. Type, then tap the send arrow. While you talk, the same box shows your words as you say them.

What your phone answers by itself

These work even when the hub does not answer. Say the command on its own, with nothing else in the sentence.

  • The flashlight: flashlight on, turn the torch off.
  • Music that is already playing: play, pause, stop music, next, skip, previous.
  • The battery: what's my battery.

What the hub answers

Everything else goes to your hub. Some examples:

  • Questions: why is the sky blue?
  • The time and date, here or anywhere: what time is it in Tokyo?
  • Money, units and dates, worked out exactly: how much is 50 euros in dollars?, what day is Christmas this year?
  • The weather where you are.
  • A ringing timer or alarm on your phone: turn that off.
  • Messages and calls to family. See People.

If you get stuck looking for a word, like the film with the dreams inside dreams, the, ΠAN may offer the word.

The assistant button

A car's voice button on the steering wheel, a headset button, or your phone's assistant gesture can open ΠAN. The first time, Android may ask which app to use. Pick ΠAN. After a press, the next thing you say goes to ΠAN for sure.

How ΠAN talks

You can give ΠAN a personality on this device. Go to Settings, Voice and sounds, Personality. Write how ΠAN should talk in How ΠAN talks, for example "calm and short", and tap Save. Turn Off brings back the usual voice.

In the same place, Sound Effects plays two soft notes when ΠAN mutes, and Vibration gives a short buzz when ΠAN starts listening or something fails. Turn either one off if you do not want it.
