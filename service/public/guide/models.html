Models

A model is a program that learned a skill from many examples, like hearing speech or answering questions. ΠAN uses a few, each for one job.

The jobs

JobWhereWhat it does
HearingPhoneTurns your speech into words, inside the app. That is why your words show on Home as you talk.
Hearing againHubThe hub writes down what you said once more, more carefully, before it answers.
Voice modelHubFollows the talk, decides whether a line was for ΠAN, and gives the quick everyday replies.
Answer modelHubA bigger thinker. The voice model hands it the harder questions.
PhotosHubLooks at a photo and says what is in it. See Showing ΠAN things.
TranslatingHubTurns one language into another. See Translating.
ΠAN's voicePhoneMakes ΠAN's voice, inside the app.

Some answers are worked out, not guessed

Money, coins, units, holidays, days of the week and the time in other towns are worked out by plain rules and fresh figures. The model only says the result. So how much is 50 euros in dollars? gets today's rate, not a guess.

Who picks the models

Your hub picks them. You do not need to choose anything. When a better model is ready, the hub can switch to it, and nothing changes in how you use ΠAN.

Your own Claude or ChatGPT Coming

For long or hard questions, you will be able to link your own Claude or ChatGPT account. ΠAN would then hand those questions to it. This is not built yet.
