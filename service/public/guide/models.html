Models

A model is a program that learned a skill from many examples, like hearing speech. ΠAN uses a few, each for one job.

The jobs

JobWhereWhat it does
HearingPhoneTurns your speech into words, so they show on Home as you talk.
Hearing againHubWrites down what you said once more, more carefully.
Voice modelHubFollows the talk, decides if a line was for ΠAN, and gives the everyday replies.
Answer modelHubA bigger thinker for the harder questions.
PhotosHubSays what is in a photo. See Showing ΠAN things.
TranslatingHubTurns one language into another. See Translating.
ΠAN's voicePhoneMakes ΠAN's voice, inside the app.

Who picks them

Your hub does. You choose nothing. When a better model is ready, the hub can switch, and nothing changes in how you use ΠAN.

Your own Claude

At the end of ΠAN Setup, you can sign in to your own Claude account. ΠAN then hands long or hard questions to it, with your details swapped out first. ΠAN never sees your password. Skip it, and ΠAN still works.
