In the car

In the car, your phone does the work and the car is its speaker. Everything works by voice.

Set it up once

  1. Connect your phone to the car's Bluetooth, the usual way.
  2. That is all. In Settings, Accessories, the car shows under Car.

On the road

  • ΠAN talks through the car's speakers and listens with the phone's own microphone. Keep the phone where it can hear you.
  • Music gets quieter while ΠAN talks, then comes back.
  • ΠAN does not put the car into a phone call.
  • To stop ΠAN mid sentence, say stop.
  • Pull down the ΠAN notification to see the last thing you said and the last thing ΠAN said. It has Mute and Stop talking too.

The voice button on the steering wheel

Many cars have a voice button. Press it once. Android may ask which app to use. Pick ΠAN. After that, a press means the next thing you say is for ΠAN, and ΠAN stops talking if it was.

ΠAN knows you are driving

When your phone is on the car's Bluetooth, or moving at car speed, ΠAN knows you are in the car. See Where ΠAN thinks you are.

Keep your eyes on the road. Talk to ΠAN; do not type to it while driving.
