In the car

In the car, your phone does the work and the car is its speaker. Everything works by voice.

Set it up once

Connect your phone to the car's Bluetooth, the usual way. That is all. The car shows in Settings, Accessories, under Car.

On the road

  • ΠAN speaks through the car and listens with the phone's microphone. Keep the phone where it can hear you.
  • Music gets quieter while ΠAN talks.
  • Say stop to stop it mid sentence.
  • The notification shows the last thing you said and the last thing ΠAN said.

The steering wheel button

Press the car's voice button once. If Android asks which app to use, pick ΠAN. After that, a press means the next thing you say is for ΠAN.

ΠAN knows you are driving when your phone is on the car's Bluetooth or moving at car speed. See On all your devices.

Keep your eyes on the road. Talk to ΠAN; do not type while driving.
