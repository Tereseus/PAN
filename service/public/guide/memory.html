Memory

ΠAN remembers what was said, who was there, and when. Ask it the way you would ask someone who was there.

What ΠAN keeps

  • What you say to ΠAN and what it says back.
  • The photos you show it.
  • The people you meet who have their own ΠAN.
  • Short notes on the things in your life that keep coming up. A private note stays out of the talk when others are around.

All of it stays on your hub at home. Nothing is kept in Don't remember or Mute. See Listening modes.

Asking

  • What did I say yesterday about the car?
  • What did we talk about this morning?
  • Do you remember what we said about the trip?

If ΠAN finds nothing, it says so.

The Memory tab

It shows everything ΠAN kept. It is locked.

  • Tap Unlock with fingerprint. On a phone with no fingerprint, tap Set a PIN once, then Unlock with PIN.
  • A tablet, car screen or TV with no fingerprint keeps Memory closed, since others may see it.
  • It locks again when you leave the tab, or when you tap the lock at the top.
  • Find things with Search a word or a name. Move between days with the arrows, or tap the date to pick one.

Forgetting

  • One thing: tap its bin icon.
  • A whole day: Delete this day.
  • Everything a search found: Delete everything found.

ΠAN asks first, then asks for your fingerprint or PIN. Tap Delete or Keep. The lines go from Home too.

Deleting removes the lines, their photos and their place in search. A summary ΠAN wrote earlier from them may still be there.

The lock itself is in Settings, Privacy: Lock Memory and Change the Memory PIN.

Everyone in your group

Each person in your group has a memory of their own. What they say to ΠAN never goes into yours.
