Memory

ΠAN remembers your talks, so you can ask about them later. You decide what it forgets.

What ΠAN keeps

What you say to ΠAN, what it says back, and the pictures it took for you. All of it is kept on your hub at home.

It keeps nothing while you are in Don't remember or Mute. See Listening modes.

Asking about the past

Just ask:

  • What did I say yesterday about the car?
  • Do you remember what we said about the trip?
  • What did we talk about this morning?

Naming a day helps ΠAN look in the right place. If it finds nothing, it says so.

The Memory tab

The Memory tab shows everything ΠAN kept. It is locked.

  • Tap Unlock with fingerprint. Nothing is shown until it opens.
  • On a phone with no fingerprint, ΠAN uses a PIN instead. The first time, tap Set a PIN. After that, tap Unlock with PIN.
  • A tablet, car screen or TV with no fingerprint keeps Memory closed. Others might see that screen.
  • Memory locks again when you leave the tab. The lock icon at the top locks it at once.

Inside:

  • The box Search a word or a name finds things.
  • The day row has arrows for the day before and the day after. Tap the date to pick a day from a calendar.
  • Each thing ΠAN kept shows its time, and a picture if there was one.

Forgetting

  • One thing: tap the bin icon on it.
  • A whole day: Delete this day.
  • Everything a search found: Delete everything found.

ΠAN asks first and says this cannot be undone. Tap Delete or Keep. It asks for your fingerprint or PIN again. The same lines also go from the talk on Home.

Deleting removes the lines, their pictures and their place in search from your hub. A summary ΠAN wrote earlier from those lines may still be there for now.

The lock settings

Go to Settings, Privacy.

  • Lock Memory is on at the start. Turning it off asks for your fingerprint or PIN.
  • Change the Memory PIN sets or changes the PIN, on a phone with no fingerprint.

Family members

Memory belongs to the person who runs the hub. When a family member talks to ΠAN on their own phone, ΠAN does not keep it in the hub owner's memory. A memory of their own, for each family member, is Coming.
