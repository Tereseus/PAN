Showing ΠAN things

Point a camera at something and ask. ΠAN looks, tells you, and remembers what it saw.

How to ask

Hold the phone with the back camera facing the thing, then say:

  • ΠAN, what am I looking at?
  • What is this?
  • Look through my phone's camera. What kind of plant is this?

Hold still. The answer takes a few seconds. ΠAN can read writing too.

After the answer

  • Ask more, like what colour is it? ΠAN still knows what the photo showed.
  • The photo shows on Home and is kept in Memory. Delete it there.
  • In Don't remember, nothing is kept.

The pendant

The pendant has its own camera, so you do not need to hold up your phone. ΠAN always says which camera a look came from.

If ΠAN says the camera is off

Turn it on in Settings, Privacy, What each device may use.
