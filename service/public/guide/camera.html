Showing ΠAN things

Point your phone at something and ask. ΠAN takes a photo, looks at it and tells you.

How to ask

  1. Hold your phone with the back camera facing the thing.
  2. Say one of these:
    • ΠAN, what am I looking at?
    • What is this?
    • Take a photo.
    • Look through my phone's camera. What kind of plant is this?
  3. Hold still for a moment. The answer takes a few seconds.

ΠAN says where the picture came from first, like From your phone's back camera: a white mug with a blue handle. It can read writing too.

After the answer

  • Ask more about it: what colour is it? ΠAN still knows what the photo showed.
  • Ask which camera did you use? and ΠAN tells you.
  • The photo shows on Home, on ΠAN's line.

Where the photos go

The photo goes to your hub, and your hub looks at it. Photos and what is in them never go to anyone else.

Photos are kept in Memory like your talks. Delete them there. In Don't remember mode, nothing is kept.

If ΠAN says the camera is off

Go to Settings, Privacy, What each device may use, and turn the camera on for this phone.
