Updates and help

The ΠAN app gets new versions from your own hub. Here is how updates work, and what to try when something is wrong.

Updates

  • Each time the app starts, it asks your hub for a newer version.
  • It downloads by itself only on Wi-Fi, and never during a call.
  • When it is ready, Android asks if you want to install the update. Say yes. Your settings stay.
  • The very first time, Android asks you to let ΠAN install apps. Allow it, then tap Check for updates again.

To check now, go to Settings, Updates and about, Check for updates. A bar shows how far the download is. The version you have is shown there too.

When something is wrong

The line at the top of Home

It saysTry this
ConnectedAll is well.
Not paired with a computer yetPair the phone. See Getting started.
No internet on this deviceCheck the phone's Wi-Fi or mobile data.
Your computer is not answeringCheck that the hub computer is on, awake and online. If it is, restart it and wait a minute.

ΠAN does not answer

  • Look at the microphone button. A crossed out microphone means muted. Tap it.
  • An ear means Listen only, and Π means Name only. Say back to normal, or hold the button and pick Normal.
  • An Interpreting badge on Home means ΠAN is translating, not answering. Tap the badge to stop.
  • A slanted note under your line, like Not for ΠAN: music was playing, tells you why it stayed quiet. Start with its name and ask again.

ΠAN answers when you did not want it to

  • Say stop, or press the power button.
  • Switch to Name only, so it answers only when you start with its name.

ΠAN mishears a name

The hub owner can add Other names for that person. See People.

Memory will not open

Use the fingerprint you set up in Android. On a phone without one, use the PIN. A tablet, car screen or TV without a fingerprint keeps Memory closed on purpose.

ΠAN says the camera is turned off

Turn the camera on in Settings, Privacy, What each device may use.

Messages from the hub

If the hub has something you should know, it shows on Home under Needs you. Read it, then tap Dismiss.

Still stuck

Go to Settings, Updates and about, Send a report. You see the whole report first. It holds the app version, the phone model, how fast it makes the voice and the last crash, and nothing you said. Tap Send and pick how to share it.
