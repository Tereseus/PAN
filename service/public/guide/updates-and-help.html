Updates and help

The ΠAN app gets new versions from your own hub. Here is how, and what to try when something is off.

Updates

  • When the app starts, it asks your hub for a newer version. It downloads only on Wi-Fi, never during a call.
  • Android asks if you want to install it. Say yes.
  • The first time, Android asks you to let ΠAN install apps. Allow it, then tap Check for updates again.

To check now: Settings, Updates and about, Check for updates. A bar shows the download.

The line at the top of Home

It saysTry this
ConnectedAll is well.
Not paired with a computer yetSee Getting started.
No internet on this deviceCheck the phone's Wi-Fi or mobile data.
Your computer is not answeringCheck the hub is on and online. If it is, restart it and wait a minute.

ΠAN does not answer

  • Look at the microphone button. A crossed out microphone means muted: tap it.
  • An ear or a Π means Listen only or Name only. Say back to normal.
  • An Interpreting badge means ΠAN is translating. Tap it to stop.
  • A slanted note under your line says why it stayed quiet. Start with its name and ask again.

ΠAN answers when you did not want it to

Say stop, or press the power button. Or switch to Name only.

Other things

  • It mishears a name: the person who set up the hub can add Other names for that person.
  • Memory will not open: use your fingerprint, or the PIN on a phone without one. Shared screens keep it closed on purpose.
  • The camera is off: Settings, Privacy, What each device may use.
  • Something under Needs you: read it, then tap Dismiss.

Still stuck

Go to Settings, Updates and about, Send a report. You see the whole report first, and it holds nothing you said. Tap Send and pick how to share it.
