People and groups

A family, a team, any group can share one ΠAN. Each person has it on their own phone.

Adding someone

This is for the person who set up the hub.

  1. In People, tap Add a person. Type their name and pick Adult, Child or Guest.
  2. Tap them, then the three dots, then Add device. A code appears.
  3. They install ΠAN and scan it. The code works once, for 10 minutes. Show it only to them.

Give your group a name with Name it. Until then it is called Family.

Messages

  • Say ΠAN, tell Mia dinner is ready. Mia's phone says your name, then says: dinner is ready.
  • ΠAN, ask Mia if she wants tea works too.
  • Or tap someone in People and type.

Read messages out loud decides if ΠAN says them or only shows them.

Calls

  • Say ΠAN, call Mia, or tap Call.
  • Mia taps Answer or Not now, or says ΠAN, answer.
  • To end, say hang up or tap End.

Ask ΠAN something in a call by starting with its name. If the answer is not personal, the other person hears it too.

Smaller groups

Inside your group you can make smaller ones, like Kids or Night shift. Pick New group in the list at the top of People, then Choose people.

Call a whole group: ΠAN, call the Kids, or ΠAN, call everyone. Others come on with Join or ΠAN, join, and go with Leave.

Choices for each person

In a conversation, tap the three dots.

  • Anyone: Don't take calls from Mia.
  • The person who set up the hub also has Other names (for a name ΠAN mishears), Messages: read out or Messages: show only, Rename and Remove.

A child

A Child can talk to ΠAN, use the camera and message people in the group. You can read a child's messages with Kit's messages. The child's phone says so. Nothing is read behind their back.

People with their own ΠAN

Under Everyone else, one of you taps Show my code and the other taps Scan a code. Then swap. Each ΠAN keeps its own record. Only the picture on the screen passes between the phones. ΠAN carries messages and calls between ΠANs too.

To drop someone, tap the three dots by their name, then Forget.
