People

Your family can use the same ΠAN, each on their own phone. You can message and call each other through it.

Mia and Kit are made up names, used as examples on this page.

Adding a person

This is for the person who runs the hub.

  1. Open the People tab and tap Add a person.
  2. Type their name and pick Adult, Child or Guest.
  3. Tap the person, then the three dots at the top, then Add device. A code appears.
  4. On their phone, install ΠAN and scan that code. The code works once, for 10 minutes. Show it only to them: whoever scans it gets their place on your ΠAN.

You can give your family or group a name with Name it. Until then it is called Family.

Messages

  • Tap someone in People, type, and tap send.
  • Or say ΠAN, tell Mia dinner is ready. Mia's phone says your name, then says: dinner is ready.
  • ΠAN, ask Mia if she wants tea works too.

The number on the People tab counts new messages.

The switch Read messages out loud decides how messages reach you. On: ΠAN says them. Off: they are only shown.

Calls

  • Tap someone, then Call. Or say ΠAN, talk to Mia or ΠAN, call Mia.
  • Their phone rings. They tap Answer or Not now, or say ΠAN, answer.
  • Then just talk. You hear each other.
  • To end, say hang up, or tap End.

A call shows at the top of every tab, so you can answer from anywhere.

During a call you can still ask ΠAN something. Start with its name. If the answer is not personal, ΠAN shares it with the other person too.

When you are muted in a call, People warns you that nobody can hear you. You still hear them.

Calling a whole group

  • Say ΠAN, call everyone, or the name of a group: ΠAN, call the Kids.
  • Or pick the group at the top of People and tap Call with the group's name.
  • Others join with Answer, Join or ΠAN, join. Tap Leave to go.

The person who runs the hub makes groups. Pick New group in the list at the top of People. Groups have Choose people, Rename and Delete. Deleting a group keeps the people.

Choices for each person

In a conversation, tap the three dots at the top.

  • Don't take calls from Mia, or Take calls from Mia again. Everyone has this.
  • The hub owner also has: Groups, Other names (up to four, for a name ΠAN mishears), Messages: read out or Messages: show only (and Let Mia choose), Add device, Rename and Remove.
  • Remove takes the person off your ΠAN and stops their devices from reaching it.

A child's device

Add the child as Child. A child can talk to ΠAN, use the camera and send messages to people on your ΠAN, and nothing else unless you open it.

As the hub owner, you can read a child's messages. In the child's conversation, tap the three dots, then Kit's messages. The child's own phone says that you can read them. Nothing is read behind their back.

People with their own ΠAN

When you meet someone who has their own ΠAN, you can keep a note of it. Under Everyone else, one of you taps Show my code and the other taps Scan a code. Then swap. Each ΠAN keeps its own record. To drop yours, tap the three dots by their name, then Forget.

Only the picture on the screen passes between the two phones. Children cannot do this.

Messages and calls with people on another ΠAN are Coming.

If messages are off for you

If People says Whoever set up this ΠAN has not opened messages for you., ask the person who runs the hub.
