Privacy

ΠAN lives on your own computer at home. Your talks, your memory and your photos stay there.

Don't remember

For a private moment, ΠAN can keep nothing at all. It still talks and answers. It keeps no lines on Home, nothing in Memory, no photos.

  • Say ΠAN, don't remember this or off the record.
  • Or hold the microphone button and pick Don't remember. The button shows a hat with glasses.

It ends only with the button. Tap or hold it and pick another mode. Nothing you say can switch memory back on by accident.

What stays home

  • Your talks, your memory and your photos are kept on your hub.
  • Your phone reaches your hub over its own private, encrypted link, made when you paired it.
  • In a call, the sound passes through your hub to the other phone. None of it is kept, and no model hears it.

Before a model reads it

ΠAN swaps out email addresses, phone numbers, card and ID numbers, street addresses and map positions first. It does this even for the models on your own hub.

What leaves home

  • When an answer needs a fresh fact, like the weather or a rate, your hub asks a public service for it, with no name on it.
  • If you linked your own Claude account, ΠAN may hand it a hard question, with your details swapped out first. See Models.

More ways to keep things out

MuteThe microphone is off. Only the button turns it back on.
IncognitoIn Settings, Privacy. What this phone sends is kept apart, and erased when you turn it off.
What each device may useIn Settings, Privacy. Turn the microphone, location, camera and other sensors on or off for each device.
Lock MemoryMemory opens only with your fingerprint or PIN.
One limit: even in Don't remember, the hub's running record, kept for fixing problems, still prints the words it heard. It is not your memory, and ΠAN never searches it.

Your group

  • What a person says to ΠAN never goes into anyone else's memory.
  • The person who set up the hub can read a child's messages. The child's phone says so.
  • They cannot open an adult's messages in People.

Sending a report

Send a report, in Settings, Updates and about, shows you the whole text first. It holds the app version, the phone model and the last crash. Nothing you said.
