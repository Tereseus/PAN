Privacy

ΠAN lives in your home. Here is what it keeps, where, and how you keep things out.

What stays home

  • Your talks with ΠAN and your memory are kept on your hub, not on a company's computers.
  • Photos you show ΠAN go to your hub, and your hub looks at them. They never go to anyone else.
  • Your phone reaches your hub over its own private, encrypted link, made when you scanned the pairing code.
  • In a call with family, the sound passes through your hub to the other phone. The hub keeps none of the sound, and no model hears it. The written lines of the call are kept in that conversation.

Scrubbed before a model reads it

Before a model reads what you said, ΠAN swaps these for placeholders: email addresses, phone numbers, card and ID numbers, street addresses, map positions and network addresses. This happens even for the models on your own hub.

What the hub asks the internet for

Some answers need fresh facts. For these, your hub asks public services for the figure, without your name:

  • The weather: the service gets your rough position.
  • Money and coin rates.
  • The time or sunrise in another town: the service gets the town's name.

Keeping things out

ChoiceWhat it does
MuteThe microphone is off. Nothing is heard. Only the button turns it back on.
Don't rememberΠAN talks as usual but keeps nothing: no lines on Home, nothing in Memory, no photos. Hold the microphone button to pick it, or say off the record. It ends with the button, or after an hour. See Listening modes.
IncognitoA switch in Settings, Privacy. While it is on, what this phone sends is kept apart from your memory. Turning it off erases it. Home shows an Incognito badge while it is on.
What each device may useIn Settings, Privacy. Pick a device and switch its microphone, location, camera and other sensors on or off. A sensor locked by your organization says why.
Lock MemoryMemory opens only with your fingerprint, or a PIN on a phone without one. See Memory.
One honest limit: even in Don't remember, the hub's own technical log on the home computer still prints the words it heard. That log is not your memory, and ΠAN does not search it.

Which to use? Don't remember is the simple one for a private moment. Incognito keeps this phone apart until you switch it off.

Family and privacy

  • When a family member talks to ΠAN on their own phone, it does not go into the hub owner's memory.
  • The hub owner can read a child's messages. The child's phone says so.
  • The hub owner cannot read an adult's messages from the People tab.

Forgetting

You can delete one thing, a whole day, or everything a search found. See Memory.

Sending a report

Send a report in Settings, Updates and about shows you the whole text before anything goes. It holds the app's version, the phone model, how fast it makes the voice, and the last crash. Nothing you said.
