What ΠAN is

ΠAN is a helper you talk to. It has a few parts, and each part has one job.

The hub

The hub is a computer in your home that runs ΠAN. It does the thinking. When you say something, the hub works out whether you were talking to ΠAN and what the answer is.

The hub also keeps the memory: what you said to ΠAN, what it said back, and the pictures you showed it. All of it is kept on the hub, in your home, not on a company's computers.

Keep the hub switched on and online. When it is off, your phone says Your computer is not answering.

The phone app

The ΠAN app runs on an Android phone. It is how you talk to ΠAN.

  • It hears you and turns your speech into words.
  • It sends the words to your hub and speaks the answer with ΠAN's own voice.
  • It shows the talk on the Home tab, so you can read it back.
  • It does a few small things by itself, like the flashlight. See Talking to ΠAN.

The app has four tabs at the bottom: Home, People, Memory and Settings.

Headphones, earbuds and glasses that work with Android also work with ΠAN. You can see them in Settings, Accessories.

The pendant Coming

The pendant is a small thing you wear. It will have a microphone and a camera, and it will talk to your phone. It is not for sale yet.

One ΠAN, a whole home

The person who sets up the hub can add family members. Each one gets ΠAN on their own phone, linked to the same hub. See People.
