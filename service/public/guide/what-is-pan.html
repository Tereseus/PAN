What ΠAN is

ΠAN remembers your life and is there on every device you use. It lives on a computer in your home, not on a company's servers.

It remembers

ΠAN keeps what was said, who was there, and when. Ask about it the way you would ask someone who was there. See Memory.

It is always there

Your phone, your car, your computers: the same ΠAN, the same memory, the same voice. See On all your devices.

It is for your group

A family or a team can share one ΠAN. Each person has it on their own phone. You message and call each other through it. See People and groups.

It translates

Talk with someone in another language. Each of you hears your own. See Translating.

It is yours

Everything lives on your hub. Say off the record and ΠAN keeps nothing until you switch it back. See Privacy.

The parts

  • The hub is a computer in your home. It does the thinking and keeps the memory. Keep it on. When it is off, your phone says Your computer is not answering.
  • The app runs on your Android phone. It hears you, speaks, and shows the talk. It has four tabs: Home, People, Memory and Settings.
  • The pendant is ΠAN to wear: ears and a camera, linked to your phone.
  • Headphones, earbuds and glasses that work with Android work with ΠAN. See them in Settings, Accessories.
