Getting started

You pair your phone with your hub once. After that, the phone finds the hub by itself.

Pair your phone

  1. When ΠAN Setup finishes on the hub computer, it opens the Pair a phone page. If you closed it, run ΠAN Setup again.
  2. Tap Show a pairing code.
  3. Open the ΠAN app on your phone. On Home, tap Scan the code and point the camera at the code.
  4. Tap Pair. The top of Home now says Connected.
A code works once, for a few minutes. Show it only to the phone you are pairing. To pair again later, go to Settings, This device, Scan a pairing code.

Turn ΠAN on

The app starts muted. Tap the round microphone button at the bottom right. Home says ΠAN is listening. Now just talk.

While ΠAN runs, a notification at the top of the screen shows if it is listening, with Mute and Stop talking buttons.

What the app asks for

PermissionWhy
MicrophoneTo hear you. Off while muted.
LocationTo know where you are, and when you are in the car.
CalendarSo ΠAN knows your plans for the next week.
Nearby devicesFor headphones, glasses and your car.
NotificationsTo show ΠAN is on, ring for calls and show messages.
CameraTo scan codes, and to show ΠAN things.
BatteryTo keep listening with the screen off.
Install appsFor updates. See Updates and help.

Change any of these for each device later in Settings, Privacy, What each device may use.

Name your phone in Settings, This device, Device Name. Your other devices see that name.
