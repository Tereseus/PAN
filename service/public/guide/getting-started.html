Getting started

You pair your phone with your hub once. After that the phone finds the hub by itself.

Pair your phone

  1. On the hub computer, open the Pair a phone page. ΠAN's setup opens it for you when it finishes. If you closed it, type localhost:7777/pair into the hub computer's web browser.
  2. Tap Show a pairing code. A square code appears.
  3. Open the ΠAN app on your phone. Android asks for a few permissions. They are explained below.
  4. On Home you see Pair this phone with your computer. Tap Scan the code.
  5. Android asks to use the camera. Allow it, then point the phone at the code on the hub's screen.
  6. The app asks if you want to pair with your hub. Tap Pair.
  7. The line at the top of Home now says Connected.
A pairing code works once, and only for a few minutes. If it runs out, tap Show a pairing code again. Show the code only to the phone you are pairing.

To pair again later, or with a new hub, go to Settings, This device, Scan a pairing code.

Turn ΠAN on

The app starts muted, so it does not listen until you say so. Home says ΠAN is muted. Tap the round microphone button at the bottom right. Now Home says ΠAN is listening. Just talk.

While ΠAN runs, Android shows a ΠAN notification at the top of the screen. It tells you whether ΠAN is listening or muted, and it has Mute and Stop talking buttons.

What the app asks for, and why

PermissionWhy ΠAN needs it
MicrophoneTo hear you. When you mute ΠAN, the microphone is off.
LocationTo answer where am I, to know the weather and daylight where you are, and to tell when you are in the car.
CalendarSo you can ask what's on my calendar. ΠAN reads your phone's own calendar for the next week.
Nearby devicesTo use Bluetooth headphones, glasses and your car.
NotificationsTo show that ΠAN is on, to ring when someone calls you, and to show messages.
CameraAsked when you first scan a code. ΠAN also uses it when you ask it to look at something.
BatteryThe app asks to be left out of battery saving, so it keeps listening with the screen off.
Install appsAsked the first time ΠAN updates itself. See Updates and help.

You can turn the microphone, location, camera and other sensors off for each device later. Go to Settings, Privacy, What each device may use.

Give your phone a name

Go to Settings, This device, Device Name. This is the name your other devices see.
