Using your computer Coming

ΠAN will help you use your computer: by voice, or with your phone as the mouse and keyboard. None of this is ready yet. This page shows the idea.

What is planned

  • A page for each computer in ΠAN, showing what it is and what ΠAN may do on it. Coming
  • Control by voice: say what you want, like open my mail, and the computer does it. Coming
  • Your phone as mouse and keyboard: use the phone's screen as a touch pad and type on its keyboard. Coming
  • The pendant as a pointer, once the pendant is out. Coming

The plan is that you choose which computers ΠAN may use, and what it may do there.

What works today

If a computer has ΠAN on it, ΠAN can tell when you are using it, from when you last typed or moved the mouse. See Where ΠAN thinks you are.

ΠAN on a computer also tells the hub the title of the window in front, like the name of the page or file you have open. ΠAN may use that title to understand what you are doing. It does not take pictures of your screen; that stays off unless someone turns it on.
