Your computers

Put ΠAN on the computers you use, and it is with you there too.

What ΠAN does on a computer

  • Knows you are there. When you type or use the mouse, ΠAN knows you are at that computer. See On all your devices.
  • Speaks and shows notes on the computer you are at.
  • Opens things. Ask for an app or a web page, and it opens on the right computer. Name the other one if it picks wrong, and ΠAN learns.
  • Plays and pauses what is playing there.

What it sees

ΠAN on a computer tells your hub the title of the window in front, like the name of the page or file you have open. It uses that to understand what you are doing.

It takes no pictures of your screen. That stays off unless you turn it on.

Your Claude threads

ΠAN can pass what you say to a Claude thread on any of your computers and read you the answer. Say "send to the music thread" and what you want.

For something long, say "message for the music thread". ΠAN says "Go" and then only listens: it keeps every word, through every pause, until you say "send it" or "over". The whole thing goes to the thread at once, and ΠAN tells you how long it was. Say "cancel the message" to drop it.

Your phone's screen

On the phones you allow, ΠAN can read the screen and tap, type and scroll for you.
