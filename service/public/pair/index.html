Pair a phone

Checking PAN Link…

The phone then reaches PAN on your PAN computer directly: over your home network at home, and through Tailscale when you're away.

  1. On the phone, open the PAN app, then Settings, then Scan a pairing code.
  2. Point the camera at the code that appears here.
  3. Tap Pair on the phone.
On this phone already? Pair in the PAN app
Pairing a computer instead?

On that computer, run pan-link pair --data <folder> --code "<this code>". The code works once, like the picture.
