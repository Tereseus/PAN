# ΠAN benches: the plan

Agreed with the owner, Nov 28. Every bench cuts across the modes (talking, doing, answering,
teaching): a turn has to hear right, decide right, do or say the right thing, safely and fast.
Each bench runs across conditions: device (phone, car unit, PC, Medallion), quiet or noisy (car,
TV), alone or with people, language. Benches that only ran on clean text are how the car's
problems (its own echo, road noise heard as words) went unseen.

Shared numbers come from made-up data only (generated by an open model, never Claude). A person's
own numbers stay on their hub unless they say yes in Reports (board task 1098).

## 3. Reply shape (intuition)
When to be an assistant, when informational, when just talking. The shape comes from the
situation (driving, alone, quick question: short and relative, said the way a person would say
it, not read out like a database). Scored without the owner reviewing logs:
- his next line labels the last reply: a correction, a repeat, cutting ΠAN off, "that's not what
  I asked" = a miss; carrying on = a hit;
- where that cannot tell: a few one tap checks a day ("chatting or asking here?"), skippable;
- for the shared bench: made-up scenes with the right shape known.
Status: missing. The intuition state (where, who is around, what he is doing) is part of the input.

## 4. Quick assist: tools and actions
Every action on every device kind: navigate, call, text, message a person, timer, alarm,
reminder, calendar, music and media, volume, flashlight, open an app, phone settings, camera
("what's this"), screen control, smart home, car. Pass: it does it, or asks first when the action
matters, or says plainly it can't; never says it can't when the device can. Status: missing
(board 1105).

## 7. Safety
Voice ID at every level, so nobody else can use a person's device (owner, Nov 28). Levels: 0 talk
and answers: voice ID. 1 timer, music, navigation: voice ID. 2 messages and emails to people: voice ID, read back, a yes. 3 money, deleting, sharing,
unlocking: the fingerprint every time (no standing approval, no payment that keeps going), plus
context: his voice, the Medallion with him, a usual place, no unknown voices around. Anything off
(unknown place, strangers, no Medallion): ask for more (a face picture) or refuse; this is also
how a person under pressure is protected. Voice ID alone is never enough for level 2 or 3.
Benches: asks before send, pay, delete (1101); memory only from his words (1102); ignores
instructions hidden in pages, emails, screens; another voice cannot give orders. Status: missing.
